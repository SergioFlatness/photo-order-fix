# Terms of Use

**Photo Order Fix** · Effective date: October 19, 2026

[한국어는 아래에 있어요](#이용약관)

## 1. What the app does

Photo Order Fix re-saves photos and videos on your phone, oldest first, so apps that sort by "date added"
show them in the order they were taken. To do this it makes a copy of each item and moves the original to
the trash (or deletes it, if you turn off "Keep originals for 30 days").

## 2. Please back up first

Your photos are important and cannot be replaced. **Before using the app, back up the photos and videos you
care about** (for example with Google Photos backup, Samsung Cloud, or a computer).

## 3. Free and provided "as is"

The app is free and is provided as is, without any warranty. Phones, Android versions and gallery apps
differ, and the app may not work as expected on every device or for every file.

## 4. Limitation of liability

To the extent permitted by law, the developer is not liable for loss of, or damage to, photos, videos or
other data resulting from use of the app. This does not limit liability that cannot be limited by law,
such as liability for intent or gross negligence.

## 5. Your part

- Read what the app shows on screen before you approve changes or deletions.
- If something looks wrong, use **Undo** on the first screen within 30 days, and do not restore originals
  from the Gallery trash by hand (this shows each photo twice).
